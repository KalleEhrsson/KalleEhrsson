### What I’m Working On

### ♟️ Chess Game (Unity)
- A **first-person chess game** where you walk around the board in 3D
- Built with an **FPS-style player controller** instead of a traditional top-down chess view
- Features a **fully AI-controlled opponent**
- Focused on combining **classic chess rules** with a more immersive game feel

### 🥽 VR Shooter
- Building **physical interaction systems** for VR
- Focus on realistic handling, body-based storage, and in-world interactions
- Designed around **immersion, clarity, and system-driven logic**
- Exploring how interactions should feel when they are more physical and less abstract
